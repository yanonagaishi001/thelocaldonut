About us

A neighborhood donut shop

We keep bringing specialty and traditional donuts to the local community and to visitors from all around Arizona. The regulars have their usual, first timers get to try something new, and everyone gets a warm hello.

Come by in the morning, grab a box for the office, or order ahead and pick it up on your way.

The fine print

Ordering, payment and refunds

Online orders, payment and pickup are handled by our Square ordering page. Square's own terms and privacy policy apply when you order there.

If something is wrong with your order, call us at (480) 500-5236 or come see us at the shop the same day and we will make it right. Refunds for online orders are issued through Square to the card you paid with.

Prices, flavors and daily availability change. What is listed here is a guide, not a guarantee that an item is in the case today.

Privacy

This website does not use cookies, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and has no forms. We do not collect any personal information from you here.

The fonts on this page load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. When you click through to our Square ordering page or our Instagram, those companies collect information under their own privacy policies.

Questions about privacy: call (480) 500-5236.
